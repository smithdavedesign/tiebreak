# tiebreak — PRD

## Problem
People stuck between two options (outfit, name, photo, thumbnail, listing image) get slow, unstructured answers from friends or forums. Sellers and creators who need a verdict pay PickFu from $15 a poll, or wait on forum threads that give no tally (https://www.pickfu.com/help/what-is-pickfu, https://community.etsy.com/t5/Etsy-Success/Which-picture-do-you-think-is-best/m-p/57028318).

## Target user
Primary payer: a solo Etsy or Amazon seller, indie author or YouTube creator choosing between 2–4 images or titles right before publishing. Secondary free user: anyone indecisive, who also supplies votes.

## Competitors & gap
PickFu is a paid panel with written reasons, at $15 or more a poll. Agree is private groups only. Wishbone is a teen entertainment app. Forums give no tally. The gap is a fast, cheap, stranger-vote swipe poll with a hard close (N votes or a deadline) and a clear verdict.

## MVP scope
- Create a poll with 2–4 options (image or text), a vote target N and a time limit.
- Swipe feed: left or right to pick between options on a card, one vote per voter per poll.
- Server-side close at N votes or the deadline, whichever comes first.
- Result page with a winner, the vote split and a shareable link.
- Share link so creators can send the poll to their own audience as well as the public feed.
- "Vote 5, post 1" credit rule to keep the feed supplied with voters.
- Report button and basic image moderation.
- Paid tier: private polls, higher vote targets and CSV export.

## Non-goals
- Native apps, demographic-vetted panels, written voter reasons, dating, political polling.

## Success metrics
- 50% of public polls reach their vote target within 24 hours in the test cohort.
- 20 sellers or creators create a poll within 30 days of posting to Etsy and creator communities.
- 3 of those 20 pay $5 or more, which would show willingness to pay below PickFu's $15.
- Median voter completes 10 or more swipes per session.

## Open questions
- Do sellers trust anonymous stranger votes enough to act on them?
- Is the credit loop enough to bootstrap voters without ads?
- What is the right price: per poll or a monthly plan?
